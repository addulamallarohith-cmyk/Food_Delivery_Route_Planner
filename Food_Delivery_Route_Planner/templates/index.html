How the Algorithms Work

Greedy / Nearest Neighbor

Repeatedly visits the closest unvisited customer. It is fast and easy to understand, but may not produce the shortest tour.

2-Opt Improvement

Rearranges route edges to remove unnecessary crossings and reduce the total travel distance.

Dijkstra

Finds a least-cost path between two locations when all connection costs are non-negative.

TSP

The delivery tour returns to the starting point after visiting every required customer.
